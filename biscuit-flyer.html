Biscuit came to us as a stray in January after nearly getting hit on the road. He has been waiting ever since. One-on-one he is a velcro dog: he stays by your side and leans his whole weight on your leg. Volunteers call him a total sweetheart in the play yard.

Don't judge him by his kennel. Biscuit gets nervous at the front of his run, so people walk past him. Ask to meet him in the play yard. That is where he shines.
